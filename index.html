Practical checks, not magic formulas
01

Watch cost growth

Exponential costs create a strong late-game wall. A small multiplier difference compounds quickly, so test the curve across the full upgrade path.

ECONOMY
02

Reward each purchase

If an upgrade barely changes income, it can feel pointless. Compare the time saved by the new income against the cost of buying it.

FEEDBACK
03

Test the slowest stretch

Average progression time can hide a miserable bottleneck. Look at the biggest jump between consecutive upgrade purchases.
